Velvet Noise

A sound machine for sleep and focus — 30+ sounds, a three-layer mixer, a 10-band equaliser and a sleep timer that fades out instead of stopping dead. Everything plays from the device; nothing streams.

Overview

Thirty-plus sounds, three layers,
and nothing that needs a signal.

Noise apps usually make you pick between range and control: a long list of loops you can only play, or a handful of sounds behind a streaming player. Velvet Noise ships the whole library on the device, then hands over an equaliser and a three-track mixer so a sound can be tuned to the room you're actually in.

01 · The brief

A sound machine, not a playlist.

The goal was an instrument rather than a library — something you set once and leave running for eight hours. That ruled out streaming, ruled out anything that stops when the screen locks, and meant every sound had to ship inside the app.

02 · The approach

One screen you never have to leave.

Library, 10-band equaliser, volume and sleep timer all sit on the playback screen, so there is no navigating in the dark at 1am. The Lab is the one place you go deeper: three layers, each with its own level, saved as a preset you can call back.

03 · In practice

It plays when nothing else can.

On a plane, in a tent, on hotel wifi — no buffering and no dropouts, because nothing is being fetched. Playback survives the screen going off, sits on the lock screen and notification shade, and fades out on a timer instead of cutting to silence.

What we shipped

Six things it does,
and a lot it deliberately doesn't.

The whole app is a playback screen plus a mixer. Everything below lives on the surface of one of those two — nothing is buried, and nothing is there to pad a feature list.

  • A library of 30+ sounds

    True white, pink and brown noise, fans and industrial hums, rain from a drizzle to a thunderstorm, cars, trains and cabin ambience, forests, rivers, ocean, fire, wind and crickets.

  • The Lab — three layers, one mix

    Stack up to three sounds at once and set each layer's level independently, then save the result as your own preset.

  • 10-band equaliser

    31 Hz through 16 kHz, so a sound can be softened, thinned out or pushed toward the low end until it suits the room.

  • Sleep timer with fade-out

    Set a duration and the audio eases down to silence rather than stopping mid-listen and waking you up.

  • Never streams

    Every sound plays from the device. No buffering, no dropouts on a plane or in a tent, and no data spent on eight hours of rain.

  • Keeps playing with the screen off

    Background playback with controls on the lock screen and notification shade, plus a favourites tab for the sounds you keep coming back to.
